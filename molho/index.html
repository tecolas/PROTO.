(02) The process

010203
FireTimeSealFire

01

Blistered over open oak.

Every chile is hand-sorted and charred over white-oak coals until the skins blister and the sugars turn to smoke. No liquid smoke. No shortcuts.

  • White oak
  • 700°F
  • 12 min char

02

Ninety days in the dark.

The mash rests in ceramic crocks with sea salt and nothing else. Wild lactobacillus does the slow work, building a depth you simply can't fake.

  • 90 days
  • 64°F
  • Wild ferment

03

Sealed by hand.Numbered by batch.

We blend, taste, adjust and seal every jar by hand — then number it, so you know exactly which fire it came from.

  • Batch-numbered
  • Glass only
  • Zero plastic
